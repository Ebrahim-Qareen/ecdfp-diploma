came from, the licence, and the attribution line that must appear under it on the page.

real case, or a real person's device.

## Rendered results — generated by our own scripts, from our own evidence

**These are not photographs and not diagrams.** Each one is a **rendering of a measured result**, produced by
the generator named beside it, so it is reproducible rather than illustrative. `D94` allows this because the
picture *is* the finding; it forbids drawing an approximation of one.

A rendered result is fingerprinted by its **pixels**, not by its file hash: any tooling that copies a PNG may
re-compress it losslessly, which moves the file hash and leaves the image identical (`D135`).

| File | Page | What it shows | Generator | Pixel SHA-256 |

| `invoice_compare.png` | — (spare) | the same invoice before and after redaction | `scripts/make_evs10.py` | — |
| `carve_compare.png` | `P09` | a fragmented photograph as the volume holds it, beside the same photograph as a header-to-footer carver returns it | `scripts/make_evs11.py` | `e279697e1e744f1db862a7415f6339cb9eb15601f91c7f50fd46d69dd3131a93` |

All three are **wide comparisons (~3:1)** and carry `class="photo wide"`, not `class="photo"` — the default
photograph width of 560 px makes each half 270 px and hides the very thing the figure exists to show
(`D134`).
