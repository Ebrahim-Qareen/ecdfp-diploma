A complete report, section by section

This is the report the course grades, written in full for a realistic case — the kind that lands on a corporate forensics desk every month: a departing employee, a client list, and a USB drive. Every section is real report text. After each one, a teaching note says why the section exists, what makes it pass the rubric, and the mistake students make there. Read the report; then read the notes; then write yours.

Teaching note

Why it exists. Every other section can be argued with; this one cannot. It is the only section with no judgement in it, which is exactly why a gap here is unforgivable — it says the examiner was careless before the analysis even started.

What passes. A unique case ID, who asked and under what reference, which machine (with an asset tag, not just a name), who examined and who reviewed, dates, a version number.

The mistake. No version number. A report that is corrected later with no version is two contradictory documents with the same title. Version 1.0 today; 1.1 the moment one word changes.

القسم الوحيد اللي مفيش فيه رأي — فمفيش عذر لأي نقص فيه. رقم الإصدار (version) إلزامي: أي تعديل بعدين = إصدار جديد، مش نفس الملف.

§2Executive summary

Legal asked whether client data was taken from the workstation assigned to a Senior Sales Manager during his notice period (11–25 May 2026). The workstation’s disk was imaged and examined; the USB drive in question was not available.

The examination establishes, with high confidence, that a Kingston DataTraveler USB drive was connected to the workstation on 19 May and again on 22 May 2026, and that on 22 May, during the logged-in session of the account assigned to the employee, the Sales client master list was opened from the network share, an archive was created on the desktop and deleted twenty minutes later, and a file carrying the client list’s name was opened from the USB drive’s drive letter. All of this happened inside a forty-minute window.

The examination does not establish that the client list was copied to the USB drive. The drive was never recovered, and without it the copy cannot be proven from the workstation alone. The evidence is consistent with a copy; it is not proof of one. Nor can the workstation evidence show which person was at the keyboard, only which account was logged in.

Two weaknesses are declared: the workstation was powered down and left at the desk overnight before it was sealed, and its memory was therefore not captured. Neither affects the integrity of the disk image, which verified. No view on intent is offered; that question is outside scope.

Recommended: recover the USB drive through lawful means if the matter proceeds; the questions this report cannot answer are answerable from the drive.

Teaching note

Why it exists. Half a page for the one reader who will not read the rest — the lawyer, the HR director, the judge. Written last, read first.

What passes. Plain language, no tool names, no paths. It says what was established, at what confidence, what was not established and why, and it declares the weaknesses itself rather than waiting for the other side to find them. Notice it never says the employee did anything — it says what the account and the device did.

The mistake. Writing it first, from what you expect to find. Then the analysis disagrees with it and the summary quietly oversells. Write it after §11, from §11.

نص صفحة لقارئ مش تقني، بتتكتب آخر حاجة و بتتقري أول حاجة. من غير أسماء أدوات ولا مسارات. و لاحظ: عمرها ما بتقول «الموظف عمل» — بتقول «الحساب» و «الجهاز».

§3Authorisation & scope

Instruction. This examination was instructed by the Head of Legal, NileBridge Logistics, on 2026-05-27 under HR case reference HR-2026-071, following an HR report of 2026-05-26 that the Sales client master list had been opened from workstation NBL-WS-117 during the notice period of its assigned user. Authority to examine company-owned equipment is under the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP-2024 §7.3) signed by all staff.

Questions this examination answers:

  1. Was a removable storage device connected to NBL-WS-117 between 2026-05-11 and 2026-05-25?
  2. Were files from the Sales client master list accessed from NBL-WS-117 in that period?
  3. Is there evidence that any such file was copied to a removable storage device?

Outside scope, on the instruction of Legal: the user’s personal devices; the corporate mailbox and cloud storage (subject to a separate legal process); any question of the user’s intent or knowledge; any device other than NBL-WS-117.

Teaching note

Why it exists. §11 is only allowed to answer the questions written here. If the questions are vague, the conclusions will be vague, and the report will drift into things nobody asked for and nobody authorised.

What passes. Who instructed you, under what authority, and a numbered list of questions specific enough that each one can be answered yes / no / not established. The out-of-scope list is as important as the in-scope one — it is what stops you being asked “why didn’t you look at his phone?”

The mistake. One question: “investigate whether the employee stole data”. That is not a question a workstation can answer, so the report answers something else and calls it that.

الأسئلة هنا لازم تبقى محدّدة لدرجة إن كل واحد يتجاوب بـ نعم / لا / لم يثبت. و «خارج النطاق» مهم زي «داخل النطاق» — ده اللي بيحميك من «ليه ما بصّيتش على موبايله؟».

Teaching note

Why it exists. This is the section the other side reads first, looking for one hole. Every transfer, two names, a time, a reason. It is the minimum SWGDE sets, and it is its own section because it is not a formality — it is the exhibit’s biography.

What passes. One line per transfer; the exhibit tracked through its transformations (unit → SSD → image → resealed); and the honest row — the declared gap. Look at how it is written: the two times either side, what is known about the gap, and a pointer to where its consequence is assessed. That row is the strongest thing in this table, not the weakest.

The mistake. Hiding the gap, or writing the table as if the examiner had the machine from the first minute. A gap you declare costs you a paragraph in §10. A gap they find costs you the exhibit.

كل نقل = سطر، اسمين، وقت، سبب. و الـ gap (الفترة اللي الجهاز كان فيها من غير حد مسؤول) بتتكتب بالوقتين اللي حواليها — مش بتتخبّى. اللي تعلنه انت بيكلّفك فقرة في قسم ١٠؛ اللي يكتشفوه هما بيكلّفك الدليل كله.

Teaching note

Why it exists. Criterion 1, Integrity, is graded here and in §4. This table is your proof that what you examined is what was seized, and that nothing changed between seizure and the last day of analysis.

What passes. Both algorithms, every time. Three moments of hashing, not one: at acquisition (the tool’s round trip), an independent check of the source (the P05 lesson — the tool’s “verified” never read the disk twice; dc3dd did), and re-verified at analysis with the command you ran. Note the derived raw export is listed but flagged as derived, and the missing device has a row — its absence is recorded, not omitted.

The mistake. One hash, computed once, from the tool’s own dialog. And omitting the exhibit you don’t have. A missing exhibit is a fact about the case; leave it out and the reader assumes you never thought about it.

الخوارزميتين، كل مرة. و تلات لحظات hash مش واحدة: عند الاقتناء، فحص مستقل للمصدر بأداة تانية (درس P05: كلمة verified ما قرتش القرص تاني)، و إعادة تحقق وقت التحليل بالأمر اللي شغّلته. و الجهاز الناقص ليه سطر — غيابه حقيقة بتتسجّل.

Teaching note

Why it exists. Criterion 2, Method: another analyst, with your image and this table, gets your result. If they cannot, your findings are your word, not evidence.

What passes. Every tool with a version. Steps in the order performed. The input path for each step (so the reader knows which hive, which user, which log). The environment stated — including the snapshot taken before mounting. And the timezone paragraph: one sentence that prevents every timestamp in §8 being wrong by three hours.

The mistake. “Used RegRipper and Eric Zimmerman’s tools.” Which plugins? Which version? On which hive? A tool without a version is not a method; it is a brand name.

§7Acquisition details

EXH-01 (workstation SSD). The SSD was received sealed in EB-0412 (§4). It was connected through a Tableau T8u forensic bridge (write-blocked; blocker log in Appendix A) to FOR-WS01. Before imaging, the device’s reported capacity was checked from Kali: hdparm --dco-identify reported no Device Configuration Overlay, and hdparm -N reported max sectors = 976773168/976773168, HPA is disabled. The image therefore covers the full native capacity.

Imaging: FTK Imager 8.3, Physical Drive source, E01 format, compression level 6, 2 GB segments (4 files), evidence number EXH-01, examiner A. Hassan, 2026-05-27 07:30–08:52 UTC. FTK Imager’s post-write verification reported matching MD5 and SHA-256. Independently of FTK Imager, the source device was then hashed from Kali with dc3dd (§5), and the two SHA-256 values are identical. A raw export was made for mounting; it carries the same hashes.

Memory. No memory image exists. NBL-WS-117 was powered down by IT Support on 2026-05-26 at HR’s request, before an examiner was engaged (§4, row 1). Volatile data from the running system is therefore unavailable (§10, L-03).

EXH-02 (USB drive). Not acquired — the device was not recovered (§4). Its identity in this report comes only from the workstation’s own records of it (F-01).

Teaching note

Why it exists, separately from §6: acquisition cannot be repeated. Method can be re-run tomorrow on the image; the moment of taking the image happened once, under conditions only you saw. So it gets its own record.

What passes. Write blocker named and logged. The capacity check before imaging (DCO then HPA — the P05 order), so the reader knows the image is complete and not 16% of a disk. The tool’s own verification and the independent hash, described as two different things. And the honest paragraphs about what was not acquired — memory, and the device — each pointing to the limitation it creates.

The mistake. “The disk was imaged and verified.” Verified by what? Against what? Covering how much of the disk? Behind what blocker? Four questions, and a defence expert will ask all of them.

الاقتناء ما بيتعادش — عشان كده ليه قسم لوحده. اكتب: الـ write blocker، فحص السعة قبل التصوير (DCO ثم HPA — ترتيب P05)، تحقق الأداة و الـ hash المستقل كحاجتين مختلفتين، و اللي ما اتاخدش (الذاكرة، الجهاز) مع الـ limitation اللي بيسببه.

Teaching note

Why it exists. Criterion 3. This is the only part of the report that is evidence. Everything after it is you.

What passes. Read F-06 again. It does not say “the file was copied to the USB drive”. It says a jump-list entry with an E:\ path existed, with a time. Read F-09: it names an account, never a person — artifacts record accounts, processes and devices, and this section keeps that rule absolutely. §9Interpretation

What the findings mean, each citing the findings it rests on, with a stated confidence and the alternative that could not be excluded.

I-01 · answers Q1

A Kingston DataTraveler 3.0 USB drive with serial 0019E06B4F27B2C1A34D0091 was connected to NBL-WS-117 on 2026-05-19 and again on 2026-05-22, the later session running from 14:03:55 to 14:41:08 UTC [F-01, F-02]. Confidence: high. The property timestamps are written by the operating system on device events; there is no indication of clock tampering (no log clears, F-10; consistent timezone, §6). Alternative not excluded: earlier connections of the same device before 2026-05-14 may have been removed from the registry by the cumulative update of that date [F-10] — the 19 May date is the earliest surviving record, not necessarily the first connection.

I-02

During the 22 May session the device was assigned drive letter E:, and its volume GUID was written into the profile of the account hossam.farid two seconds after arrival, while that account’s interactive session (08:31–16:02 UTC) was the only one open [F-03, F-04, F-09]. Confidence: high that the device was used under that profile. This does not establish which person was at the keyboard; MountPoints2 attributes a device to a logged-in profile, not to a person. Alternative not excluded: another person using the unlocked session (§10, L-02).

I-03 · answers Q2

The Sales client master list was opened from the network share at 14:12 UTC, and a file with the same name was opened from E:\ seven minutes later [F-05, F-06]. Confidence: high that both accesses occurred. The sequence — share, then 7-Zip, then an archive, then the file at E:\ — is consistent with the file being copied to the device in this session. Alternative not excluded: a file of that name was already on the device and was opened from it, without a copy occurring on this workstation.

I-04 · bears on Q3

An archive clients.7z of 2,063,884 bytes was created on the desktop 38 seconds after 7-Zip ran, and was moved to the Recycle Bin twenty minutes later, three minutes before the device was removed [F-07, F-08, F-02]. Its size is consistent with the 2,081,792-byte spreadsheet after 7-Zip compression (an already-compressed .xlsx shrinks by about 1%). Confidence: medium that the archive contained the client list — the size fits, the name fits, the timing fits, but the archive’s contents could not be recovered (§10, L-07). Alternative not excluded: an archive made for a legitimate handover of the same file, deleted when no longer needed.

Teaching note

Why it exists. Criterion 4, Separation. This is where you are finally allowed to think out loud — and every sentence has to show which findings it stands on.

What passes. The three parts, every time: the claim, citing findings in brackets; a confidence in words you would defend; and the alternative you could not exclude. Look at I-03: the phrase is “consistent with”, and it is followed immediately by the alternative. That phrase is the most useful in forensic writing, because it is honest about the gap between what was seen and what it means. Notice the italics and the dashed border: the course marks interpretation visually so no reader mistakes it for §8.

The mistake. An interpretation with no bracketed finding under it — opinion floating free. Or a confidence stated as “certain”. Or no alternative, which tells the reader you did not look for one.

هنا مسموح تفكّر بصوت عالي — بس كل جملة لازم تبيّن واقفة على أنهي findings. التلات أجزاء كل مرة: الادّعاء [مع أرقام الـ F]، درجة الثقة، و البديل اللي ما قدرتش تستبعده. أهم عبارة: «consistent with» — و بعدها البديل على طول.

§10Limitations and what could not be determined

L-01 · the device

EXH-02 was not recovered. Nothing on EXH-01 can show that a file was written to the device. F-06 shows a file was opened from E:\; that is consistent with a copy in this session and equally consistent with a file already present. Question 3 cannot be answered from the workstation alone.

L-02 · the person

The evidence attributes activity to the account hossam.farid and its open session, not to a person. Screen-lock and unlock events were not audited on this system, so use of the unlocked session by someone else between 08:31 and 16:02 UTC cannot be excluded.

L-03 · memory

No memory image exists (§7). Any evidence that existed only in RAM — open windows, clipboard content, the 7-Zip process and its arguments — is lost.

L-04 · the custody gap

The unit was unattended at the desk overnight (§4). The disk image verified against an independent source hash, so the data examined is what was on the disk on 27 May; but physical access to the machine during the gap, including powering it on, cannot be excluded. No logon or power event was recorded in that window (F-09, F-10), which reduces but does not remove this concern.

L-05 · registry completeness

Windows can remove USB device records during cumulative updates; one was applied on 2026-05-14 (F-10). Device connections before that date may not be reflected in USBSTOR. The window before 14 May is therefore weaker than the window after it.

L-06 · no partition events

Microsoft-Windows-Partition%4Diagnostic.evtx held no records (F-10), so no partition-level insertion events corroborate the registry timestamps in F-02.

L-07 · the archive

The contents of clients.7z could not be recovered: its MFT record is not in use and its data clusters were reallocated to other files (MFTECmd, Appendix D). Its size and name are known; its contents are not.

L-08 · intent

Whether any action was deliberate, authorised, or for what purpose is not something these artifacts can show, and it is outside scope (§3).

Teaching note

Why it exists. Graded, and the section most new examiners leave thin. A report with no limitations reads as one that did not look. A report with honest limitations reads as one you can trust on everything else.

What passes. Each limitation says what could not be determined, why (which artifact was missing or ambiguous), and what it does to the conclusions. L-01 is the most important line in this report: it is the reason §11 answers question 3 with “not established”. L-04 shows how to handle the declared custody gap — it neither dismisses it nor lets it swallow the case; it says exactly what the hash does and does not protect against.

The mistake. One generic line — “further analysis may reveal more”. That is not a limitation; it is a shrug. And the opposite mistake: burying a limitation in §9 as a subordinate clause, hoping it reads as a detail rather than a gap.

القسم اللي المبتدئين بيسيبوه فاضي، و هو اللي بيخلّي الباقي يتصدّق. كل limitation: إيه اللي ما اتحددش، ليه (أنهي artifact ناقص أو غامض)، و أثره على الاستنتاج. L-01 هي أهم سطر في التقرير كله — هي سبب إن سؤال ٣ إجابته «لم يثبت».

§11Conclusions

Question 1 — Was a removable storage device connected to NBL-WS-117 between 2026-05-11 and 2026-05-25?
Yes. A Kingston DataTraveler 3.0 with serial 0019E06B4F27B2C1A34D0091 was connected on 2026-05-19 and on 2026-05-22 from 14:03 to 14:41 UTC (I-01). Connections before 14 May cannot be ruled out or in (L-05).

Question 2 — Were files from the Sales client master list accessed from NBL-WS-117 in that period?
Yes. Clients_Master_2026.xlsx was opened from \\NBL-FS01\Sales\Master at 14:12 UTC on 2026-05-22, and a file of that name was opened from drive E: at 14:19 UTC, under the account hossam.farid during its open session (I-02, I-03). Which person performed these actions is not established (L-02).

Question 3 — Is there evidence that any such file was copied to a removable storage device?
Not established. The evidence is consistent with a copy of the client list to the USB drive during the 22 May session — the file was opened from the device’s drive letter seven minutes after being opened from the share, and an archive of matching size was created and deleted in the same window (I-03, I-04). Without the device (L-01) the copy cannot be proven from the workstation, and the alternative explanations in I-03 and I-04 remain open.

No conclusion is offered on intent (L-08). No finding in this report attributes an action to a named person.

Teaching note

Why it exists. To answer §3 — and nothing else. No new facts, no new opinions. If it is not in §8 or §9 already, it cannot appear here.

What passes. The questions restated word for word, each answered yes / no / not established, each answer pointing back to the interpretations and limitations it rests on. Question 3 is the one to study: the honest answer is not “no” and not “yes”. It is “not established”, with the evidence for it laid out and the reason it falls short named. That answer is stronger in front of a lawyer than a confident “yes” would be, because it cannot be knocked down.

The mistake. “It is concluded that the employee exfiltrated the client list.” Three failures in one sentence: a person named, a verb the evidence does not support, and a conclusion to a question nobody asked. You are not the judge.

بيجاوب على أسئلة قسم ٣ و بس — مفيش حقايق جديدة ولا آراء جديدة. سؤال ٣ هو اللي تتعلّم منه: الإجابة الصادقة مش «نعم» و مش «لا» — هي «لم يثبت»، مع الدليل اللي معاك و السبب اللي بيخلّيه ناقص. الإجابة دي أقوى قدام محامي من «نعم» واثقة، لأنها ما بتتهدّش.
