This is the syllabus, not the session. The teaching pages publish one session at a time in Phase 4. When S1 ships, its card on the home page links to the teaching page and this brief stays here as the syllabus.

What you will be able to do

By the end of the session, unaided, at your own keyboard.

  • State what an investigator may claim from evidence — and what they may not.
  • Compute and verify MD5 and SHA-256, and say precisely what a match does and does not prove.
  • Complete a chain-of-custody form so a third party can reconstruct the handling without asking you.
  • Show that a write-blocker was in use, rather than assert it.
  • Write the fixed report with findings and interpretation visibly separate.

Opens from

Nothing — this is the first session. You arrive with the analyst workstation imported and booted once.

Closes with

A verified manifest, a completed chain-of-custody form, and the report template you will use five more times.

What you hand in

One report, graded on the same four criteria as every other session. The rubric never changes — that is what makes your progress measurable. The report and its rubric →

CriterionWhat is graded
1 · IntegrityHashes recorded before and after, chain of custody complete, working copy demonstrated.
2 · MethodWhat you did, with which tool and version, in an order another analyst could repeat.
3 · FindingsFact only. Every finding names its artifact and gives the exact path. No verbs of inference.
4 · SeparationInterpretation kept visibly distinct from findings, and the limitations stated outright.

Deliverable — Chain-of-Custody Record

One unbroken custody record runs across the whole course. This session's record carries every earlier session forward and adds its own — the authorization gate, this session's custody steps, and the running transfer log. Fill it as you work; the exported PDF/HTML is the record you submit. Structure follows SWGDE 18-Q-002, SWGDE 18-F-002 and ISO/IEC 27037. Open the S1 Chain-of-Custody record →

It is one record, not six. The custody-transfer log and the disposition of originals are case-level — clearing a session never erases them. You do not silently erase a chain.
