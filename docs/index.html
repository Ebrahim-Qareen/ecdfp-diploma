The distinction this course is built on

Separating what the evidence says from what you conclude is the professional skill eCDFP exists to teach. It is colour-coded on every page and it is criterion 4 of the report rubric. The border style carries the same meaning as the colour, so the distinction survives a bad projector, a greyscale printout and colour blindness.

Finding

SYSTEM\MountedDevices records volume GUID {…} for a device with serial 07A1….

Interpretation

A removable device was attached to this host at least once while this hive was live.

Cannot prove

Who attached it, or that any file was copied to it. That needs a different artifact.

About this course

Who it is for

Analysts who have finished SOC Analyst, CEH or eCIR and now have to answer the question those courses stop at: what actually happened on this machine, and how do you know? Windows, networking and command-line fundamentals are assumed, never re-taught.

How it works

Every session is the same shape: 130 minutes of theory going straight into your own keyboard, 60 minutes of blocked independent investigation, and 15 minutes of hash-verify and chain-of-custody close. You work alone at the keyboard; you pair only to peer-review a report.

What you leave with

A verified acquisition you made yourself, six graded reports against one unchanging rubric, a capstone investigation across disk, memory and network — and the habit of saying this artifact cannot prove that out loud.
