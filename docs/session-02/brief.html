S2 — Acquisition — Disk, Memory & Live Response

Order of volatility, memory capture, FTK Imager and dc3dd, E01 vs raw vs AD1. The carry-through case is born here.

This is the syllabus, not the session. The teaching pages publish one session at a time in Phase 4. When S2 ships, its card on the home page links to the teaching page and this brief stays here as the syllabus.

What you will be able to do

By the end of the session, unaided, at your own keyboard.

  • Collect volatile data in an order you can defend, and name what each step destroys.
  • Acquire memory before anything touches the disk, and state the pitfalls of the tool you used.
  • Choose between E01, raw and AD1 for a given job and justify the choice.
  • Image and verify a suspect USB end to end, hash before and after.
  • Read the partition and file-system structure of the image you just made.

Opens from

S1 — you can hash, you can document custody, and you have a report template.

Closes with

The suspect USB acquired and verified, and the first image of EVI-SRC01 in your hands.

What you hand in

One report, graded on the same four criteria as every other session. The rubric never changes — that is what makes your progress measurable. The report and its rubric →

CriterionWhat is graded
1 · IntegrityHashes recorded before and after, chain of custody complete, working copy demonstrated.
2 · MethodWhat you did, with which tool and version, in an order another analyst could repeat.
3 · FindingsFact only. Every finding names its artifact and gives the exact path. No verbs of inference.
4 · SeparationInterpretation kept visibly distinct from findings, and the limitations stated outright.

Deliverable — Chain-of-Custody Record

One unbroken custody record runs across the whole course. This session's record carries every earlier session forward and adds its own — the authorization gate, this session's custody steps, and the running transfer log. Fill it as you work; the exported PDF/HTML is the record you submit. Structure follows SWGDE 18-Q-002, SWGDE 18-F-002 and ISO/IEC 27037. Open the S2 Chain-of-Custody record →

It is one record, not six. The custody-transfer log and the disposition of originals are case-level — clearing a session never erases them. You do not silently erase a chain.
