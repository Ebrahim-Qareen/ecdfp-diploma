Practice labs

One lab per page, and every one is free — a room verified free on TryHackMe or CyberDefenders, a picoCTF challenge, a free reference to read, or an exercise on evidence you already hold. Each is the task on that page; each ends in something you write. Where a room is premium, the course says so and gives you the free route instead.

P01Foundations & the deliverable
LabownHash two tools from C:\Forensics\Tools\, compare to TOOLS.sha256, record the tool version from the file, not the folder name.
PlusreadNIST SP 800-86, section 3 — one line on what it adds to the page.
Hand inReport §1, §3, §6 — and one F-01 / I-01 / cannot-prove line. §6 is what is graded.
P02Evidence integrity
LabTHMForensic Imaging — Easy · 45 min. The mount tasks: mount read-only (ro,noload), hash before and after.
Hand inReport §4 and §5 for the room’s image — both algorithms, the FAILED line explained.
P03Data at byte level
LabownSix to eight files of your own: xxd -l 16 + file + exiftool on each. At least one must come back “no metadata”.
PlusreadGary Kessler · File Signatures and the ExifTool docs.
Hand inReport §8 findings — each with its artifact, exact path, and the measurement in the tool’s own words.
P04Volatile evidence
LabTHMVolatility Essentials — Volatility 3, no profiles.
PlusCDReveal — Easy · 45 min, a Windows memory image.
Hand inReport §4 and §6 for the capture, plus the four memory questions answered.
Caveat you must write: windows.cmdline reads the PEB, which the process itself can rewrite — a command line is a lead, not proof.
P05Acquisition
LabownImage a USB stick you own with FTK Imager 8.3, then step 8: sha256sum on the source device itself, compared to the image.
PlusTHMForensic Imaging — the imaging vocabulary and formats.
Hand inReport §5 filled, plus one paragraph: what a defence expert would say about your log. That paragraph is the assessment.
No free lab anywhere teaches acquisition as a decision — every one starts “you have been provided with an image”. That is why you image your own device: you know the ground truth.
P06Hidden information
LabownThe seven EVS-10 files: the four checks in cost order. Then a photo from your own phone, opened and re-saved — compare the three timestamps before and after.
PluspicopicoCTF · Forensics — one metadata or stego challenge, in the browser.
Hand inReport §8 (what is present) and §9 (what you assess) — without one word on why it is there.
P07Malicious files
LabownAny .docx renamed to .zip and listed; any ordinary .exe walked from e_lfanew to its imports. Nothing downloaded, nothing run.
PlusCDObfuscated — Medium · 1h30. VBA/JavaScript in a Word document with oledump and olevba.
Hand inOne finding written as fact beside the sentence you were tempted to write and could not support; the IOC report from the room.
Obfuscated contains a real sample. Isolated VM, static tools only — never open it in Word, never run it.
P08The disk
LabownYour own cluster size (fsutil fsinfo ntfsinfo C: / stat -f .); then EVS-07-wiped.dd: scan for boot sectors, discard the third candidate with the BPB_BkBootSec evidence, rebuild the table.
PlusTHMMBR and GPT Analysis — Medium · 80 min. Stretch: FAT32 Analysis, Hard · 90 min.
Hand inThe recovered table, and the candidate you discarded and why — the line that separates a forensic recovery from an IT one.
P09File systems
LabownEVS-11: read deleted MFT record 67; recover the 0xE5 FAT entry from its first cluster; carve EVS-11-carve.dd with foremost or photorec.
PlusTHMFAT32 Analysis — allocation, directory entries, deletion semantics.
Hand inA carve table: each file exact / short / over, and why — the limits of carving written as facts, not hidden.
TryHackMe’s File Carving and NTFS Analysis rooms are premium; carving is practised with free tools on the image you hold, which is better anyway — you know the ground truth.
P10The registry
LabownExport your own SYSTEM hive (reg save HKLM\SYSTEM C:\temp\SYSTEM.hiv) and run compname, timezone, usbstor; then one device in EVS-12, its full chain serial → letter → GUID → user.
PlusTHMWindows Forensics 1 — Medium · 60 min, the registry as an evidence source.
Hand inFour timestamps on one timeline, timezone-corrected — and which of them is actually the earliest.
P11What ran, what was touched
LabownEVS-13: PECmd on CHROME vs CALC; the ShimCache line for PowerShell.exe and why its date is not a run time.
PlusCDSysinternals — Medium · 1h, ShimCache with AppCompatCacheParser.
Hand inThe artifact → one-verb table for everything you opened, and every upgrade you refused.
The Sysinternals lab treats ShimCache as execution evidence and never says otherwise. ShimCache records presence, not execution. Correct the lab’s claim in your report — that is the exercise.
P12Windows event logs
LabownEVS-14: filter each .evtx to the EventID that matters (never scroll), write each event as EventID + Provider + UTC + the one field, map to MITRE.
PlusTHMCompromised Windows Analysis — Easy · 75 min; the attacker clears every log, exactly as in the page.
Hand inThe six events on one timeline with MITRE techniques, fact separated from inference.
P13Network evidence
LabownEVS-08.pcap with tshark: prove the 30-second beacon, decode the DNS TXT tunnel, find the POST; read the email header; tie the one IP across both.
PlusCDPacketMaze — Medium · 45 min, multi-protocol traffic in Wireshark, NetworkMiner and Brim.
Hand inThe intrusion as one story — delivery → C2 → exfil — each claim tied to a packet or header field, mapped to MITRE.
P14Timelines & the final report
LabownThe capstone: from the EVS-09 super-timeline, write §8 Findings and §10 Limitations of the report, then review a classmate’s against one question — is any claim one adjective past its evidence?
PlusTHMIR Timeline Analysis — Medium · 60 min, best done before the capstone.
Hand in§8 and §10, plus the peer review. Model it on the worked report example.

How “free” was checked. Every TryHackMe and CyberDefenders room here was verified against the platform’s own free/premium status when the course was built (the working lists are in the course’s Resources/LABS notes). Platforms change their tiers; if a room asks you to pay, tell the instructor and use the own exercise on that page — it is never optional, the room is.
