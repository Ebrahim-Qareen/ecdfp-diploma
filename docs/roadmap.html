Open items

The roadmap above was built on 28 August, before the knowledge base was condensed and before decisions D35–D44 were taken, so it cannot contain them. That is drift, and the honest response is to declare it rather than quietly re-cut a locked budget. Nothing below moves a minute — every session still totals 220. Three classes: decided a decision names it and no topic row carries it yet · case-critical the carry-through incident or a case question needs evidence no topic teaches · source a topic in the map has no INE source and must be researched or removed.
