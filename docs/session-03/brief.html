S3 — Data Representation & File Examination

Hex, magic bytes, headers and footers, EXIF and metadata, malicious documents, signature versus extension.

This is the syllabus, not the session. The teaching pages publish one session at a time in Phase 4. When S3 ships, its card on the home page links to the teaching page and this brief stays here as the syllabus.

What you will be able to do

By the end of the session, unaided, at your own keyboard.

  • Read a file in hex and locate its header and footer.
  • Identify a file by structure rather than by extension.
  • State what a signature/extension mismatch proves — and what it only suggests.
  • Read EXIF and document metadata critically, including how far to trust a timestamp.
  • Describe a malicious document’s structure — OLE/OOXML, embedded objects, macro storage — safely.

Opens from

S2 — a verified image, and the discipline of working only on a copy.

Closes with

Twelve files identified by header, and the carry-through malicious document opened and described without executing it.

What you hand in

One report, graded on the same four criteria as every other session. The rubric never changes — that is what makes your progress measurable. The report and its rubric →

CriterionWhat is graded
1 · IntegrityHashes recorded before and after, chain of custody complete, working copy demonstrated.
2 · MethodWhat you did, with which tool and version, in an order another analyst could repeat.
3 · FindingsFact only. Every finding names its artifact and gives the exact path. No verbs of inference.
4 · SeparationInterpretation kept visibly distinct from findings, and the limitations stated outright.

Deliverable — Chain-of-Custody Record

One unbroken custody record runs across the whole course. This session's record carries every earlier session forward and adds its own — the authorization gate, this session's custody steps, and the running transfer log. Fill it as you work; the exported PDF/HTML is the record you submit. Structure follows SWGDE 18-Q-002, SWGDE 18-F-002 and ISO/IEC 27037. Open the S3 Chain-of-Custody record →

It is one record, not six. The custody-transfer log and the disposition of originals are case-level — clearing a session never erases them. You do not silently erase a chain.
