# P01 · Independent work

**`T01` · 12 min · individual.** Two sorting exercises. Nobody tells you the answer first — that is
the point.
<span class="ar">**تمرينين فرز، لوحدك.** محدش هيقولك الإجابة الأول — و دي الفكرة.</span>

---

## (a) May write / may not write · 4 min

Six statements. Sort each into the column it belongs in, and **write one line saying why.**
<span class="ar">ست جمل. حط كل واحدة في العمود بتاعها، و **اكتب سطر واحد يقول ليه**.</span>

| # | Statement |
|--:|---|
| 1 | `Prefetch` recorded `RCLONE.EXE` as executed three times, last at `2026-08-25 21:14:07 UTC`. |
| 2 | The user `l.bennett` copied the customer database to a USB device. |
| 3 | No file named `export.7z` was present in the collected file set. |
| 4 | The file `export.7z` was never on the machine. |
| 5 | The process `svchost.exe` from `C:\Users\...\Temp\` was running when memory was captured. |
| 6 | The malware was running at the time of the compromise. |

**Success criteria** — you have finished when:

- every statement is in a column
- each has a one-line reason
- **your reasons do not all say the same thing.** Three of these fail for different reasons: one
  names a person, one claims absence rather than non-observation, one extends a fact from the time
  of collection to a time nobody observed
<span class="ar">**علامة إنك خلصت:** كل جملة في عمود · كل واحدة معاها سبب سطر · و **الأسباب مش كلها نفس السبب**. تلاتة منهم بيفشلوا لأسباب مختلفة: واحدة بتسمّي شخص، واحدة بتدّعي الغياب بدل عدم الملاحظة، و واحدة بتمد حقيقة من وقت الجمع لوقت محدش لاحظه.</span>

---

## (b) Admissible or not — and whose failure? · 8 min

Five scenarios. For each: **admissible or not**, and **whose failure is it** — the examiner's, the
lawyer's, or nobody's.
<span class="ar">خمس سيناريوهات. لكل واحدة: **مقبولة ولا لأ**، و **غلطة مين** — المحلل، ولا المحامي، ولا محدش.</span>

| # | Scenario |
|--:|---|
| 1 | A video that identifies the suspect is recovered flawlessly. The warrant covered text documents only. |
| 2 | The disk image verifies against its acquisition hash. The examiner cannot say who handed them the drive or when. |
| 3 | The report states *"the attacker used a stolen account."* The evidence shows a successful logon from that account at that time, and nothing else. |
| 4 | A perfect acquisition of a laptop belonging to a person not named in the authorisation. |
| 5 | The examiner opened three files on the original drive before imaging it, and recorded that they did. |

**Success criteria** — you have finished when:

- each has a verdict and an owner
- **at least one is "nobody's failure"** — if all five are somebody's fault, re-read the three tests
- for scenario 5 you have decided whether *recording it* changes the answer, and can say why
<span class="ar">**علامة إنك خلصت:** كل واحدة ليها حكم و صاحب · **واحدة على الأقل "مش غلطة حد"** — لو الخمسة كلهم غلطة حد، ارجع اقرا التلات اختبارات · و في السيناريو ٥ تكون قررت هل **إنه سجّلها** بيغيّر الإجابة، و تقدر تقول ليه.</span>

## T02 · Write your own triple · 5 min

From this artifact line, write **F-01**, **I-01** and one **cannot prove** line, in the fixed
formats.
<span class="ar">من سطر الأثر ده، اكتب **F-01** و **I-01** و سطر **cannot prove** واحد، بالصيغ الثابتة.</span>

> `Amcache.hve` on `FIN-WKS-07`, key `Root\InventoryApplicationFile\rclone.exe|...`,
> SHA-1 `a1b2c3…`, entry written `2026-08-25 20:49:11 UTC`, path
> `C:\Users\l.bennett\Downloads\rclone.exe`.

Then **swap with the person next to you** and mark theirs against criteria 3 and 4 of the rubric.
This is the only pairing on this page — every investigation in this course is individual.
<span class="ar">بعدين **بدّل مع اللي جنبك** و صحّح بتاعه على معيار ٣ و ٤ من الـ rubric. دي المرة الوحيدة اللي بتشتغلوا فيها اتنين — كل تحقيق في الكورس ده فردي.</span>

**Success criteria:**

- **F-01** survives the *because* test and contains no banned word
- **I-01** cites F-01, states a confidence, and names an alternative it could not exclude
- the cannot-prove line says something the artifact genuinely does not show — **and `Amcache` gives
  you an easy one, if you know what it does not record**

**Time box: 5 minutes.** If you are still writing at 5, hand in what you have — an unfinished
finding is more useful to mark than a polished one you did not write yourself.
